# TC04 — Attempt to upload a PDF while offline

Suite: Offline and Network Settings

Verify that an upload attempted without connectivity is not incorrectly reported as completed and that the app communicates an offline, pending, or unsuccessful state.

## Preconditions

1. Environment: ENV-001 — Samsung Galaxy S24 FE, Android 16, One UI 8.5, Google Drive 2.26.387.

2. The user is signed in to Google Drive.

3. The Google account has sufficient available storage.

4. A readable PDF smaller than 1 MB is stored locally as TC04_R01_document.pdf.

5. The destination folder QA-Connectivity-Lab exists and contains no file with that name.

6. No other uploads are pending.

7. The desktop browser has independent internet access and is signed in to the same Google account.

8. The device's initial connectivity and Wi-Fi-only transfer settings are recorded for restoration after the test.

## Procedure

| Step | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | While connected, open QA-Connectivity-Lab in the Android app. | The destination folder opens successfully. |
| 2 | Disable both Wi-Fi and mobile data on the phone. Ensure no alternative internet connection is active. | The phone has no active internet connection. |
| 3 | Attempt to upload TC04_R01_document.pdf from local storage. | The app does not report a successful upload. It indicates that the operation is offline, waiting, pending, or unsuccessful. |
| 4 | Observe the app for 30 seconds and record the exact message and upload state. | The app does not falsely indicate completion or crash. The operation's incomplete state remains identifiable. |
| 5 | Keeping the phone offline, refresh the destination folder in the desktop browser. | TC04_R01_document.pdf is not present in the cloud folder. |
| 6 | Open the original PDF from local storage on the phone. | The original PDF remains available and readable. |

## Postconditions

1. Capture the offline status and desktop verification before restoring connectivity.

2. Cancel the pending upload if that option is available.

3. Restore the device's initial connectivity settings.

4. If the upload completes after reconnection, remove only TC04_R01_document.pdf from the test folder after evidence collection.

5. Ensure no upload from this case remains pending.
