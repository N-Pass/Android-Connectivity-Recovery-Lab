# TC13 — Maintain a consistent upload state after locking and unlocking the screen

Suite: App Lifecycle and Retry

Verify that an upload remains recoverable and its status is consistent after the device screen is locked and unlocked.

## Preconditions

1. Environment: ENV-001 — Samsung Galaxy S24 FE, Android 16, One UI 8.5, Google Drive 2.26.387.

2. The user is signed in to Google Drive.

3. The Google account has sufficient available storage.

4. A playable MP4 video without personal information is stored locally as TC13_R01_video.mp4.

5. The video is large enough for the upload to remain active until the screen is locked. Start with approximately 20–50 MB and adjust if needed.

6. The destination folder QA-Connectivity-Lab exists and contains no file with that name.

7. No other uploads are pending.

8. Wi-Fi is connected with working internet access. Mobile data is disabled.

9. The desktop browser has independent internet access and is signed in to the same Google account.

10. The device's power-saving state, Google Drive battery restrictions, charging state, and initial connectivity and transfer settings are recorded.

## Procedure

| Step | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open the local video. Record its file size and duration, and check playback at the beginning, middle, and end. | The video plays correctly. Reference details are recorded for comparison. |
| 2 | Open QA-Connectivity-Lab and start uploading TC13_R01_video.mp4 over Wi-Fi. | The app shows that the upload is in progress. |
| 3 | While the upload is active and Google Drive is in the foreground, lock the screen using the device's side button. Record the time. | The screen turns off and the device is locked. |
| 4 | Leave the device locked for 30 seconds without intentionally changing connectivity. | The planned locked-screen interval is completed. |
| 5 | Unlock the device and return to Google Drive if necessary. Record the upload state, any message, and whether Wi-Fi is still connected. | The app is usable and shows an identifiable upload state, such as in progress, pending, unsuccessful, or completed. |
| 6 | If the upload is incomplete, observe whether it continues. If necessary, use an available retry action without creating another active upload. Record any manual intervention. | The upload continues or can be recovered through a clear user action. |
| 7 | After the app indicates completion, refresh QA-Connectivity-Lab in the desktop browser. | Exactly one file named TC13_R01_video.mp4 is listed, consistent with the completed status in the app. |
| 8 | Download the uploaded video on the desktop. Compare its size and duration with the original, and check playback at the beginning, middle, and end. | File size and duration match the original. The checked sections play without visible or audible corruption. |
| 9 | Open the original video on the phone again. | The local original remains available and playable. |

## Postconditions

1. Keep the uploaded file and collected evidence until review is complete.

2. Ensure no upload from this case remains pending.

3. Restore any device settings changed for this test after evidence collection.
