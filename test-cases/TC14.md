# TC14 — Recover upload state after dismissing Google Drive from recent apps

Suite: App Lifecycle and Retry

Verify that Google Drive displays a consistent upload state and allows completion after it is dismissed from recent apps during an active upload and reopened.

## Preconditions

1. Environment: ENV-001 — Samsung Galaxy S24 FE, Android 16, One UI 8.5, Google Drive 2.26.387.

2. The user is signed in to Google Drive.

3. The Google account has sufficient available storage.

4. A playable MP4 video without personal information is stored locally as TC14_R01_video.mp4.

5. The video is large enough for the upload to remain active until Google Drive is dismissed from recent apps. Start with approximately 20–50 MB and adjust if needed.

6. The destination folder QA-Connectivity-Lab exists and contains no file with that name.

7. No other uploads are pending.

8. Wi-Fi is connected with working internet access. Mobile data is disabled.

9. The desktop browser has independent internet access and is signed in to the same Google account.

10. The device's power-saving state, Google Drive battery restrictions, and initial connectivity and transfer settings are recorded.

## Procedure

| Step | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open the local video. Record its file size and duration, and check playback at the beginning, middle, and end. | The video plays correctly. Reference details are recorded for comparison. |
| 2 | Open QA-Connectivity-Lab and start uploading TC14_R01_video.mp4 over Wi-Fi. | The app shows that the upload is in progress. |
| 3 | While the upload is active, open recent apps and swipe away only the Google Drive card. Do not use Force stop or Clear all. | The Google Drive card is removed from recent apps. |
| 4 | Wait 30 seconds, keeping the screen on and Wi-Fi connected. | The observation interval is completed without an intentional connectivity change. |
| 5 | Reopen Google Drive using its app icon and navigate to QA-Connectivity-Lab. Record any upload status or message. | The app opens successfully and the destination folder is accessible. |
| 6 | Refresh the destination folder in the desktop browser and compare it with the Android app. | The observations allow identification of whether the upload completed, remains incomplete, or needs a new attempt. The app does not falsely report completion. |
| 7 | If the upload is incomplete, allow any active transfer to continue. If no transfer is active, use an available retry or upload action. Record the action taken and avoid starting a duplicate active upload. | The upload continues or can be completed through a clear user action. |
| 8 | After completion, refresh the destination folder on the desktop again. | Exactly one file named TC14_R01_video.mp4 is listed, with no pending upload or error indicator in the Android app. |
| 9 | Download the uploaded video on the desktop. Compare its size and duration with the original, and check playback at the beginning, middle, and end. | File size and duration match the original. The checked sections play without visible or audible corruption. |
| 10 | Open the original video on the phone again. | The local original remains available and playable. |

## Postconditions

1. Keep the uploaded file and collected evidence until review is complete.

2. Ensure no upload from this case remains pending.

3. Restore any device settings changed for this test after evidence collection.
